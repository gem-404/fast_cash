Last Updated: January 2025

We collect personal information when you register for our services, including:

  • Full name and contact details
  • National ID information
  • M-Pesa phone number
  • Financial transaction history
  • Device and usage information

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information to:

  • Process loan applications and transactions
  • Verify your identity and prevent fraud
  • Provide customer support
  • Improve our services and develop new features
  • Comply with legal and regulatory requirements

3. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encryption, access controls, and secure servers to protect your information.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Credit reference bureaus
  • Regulatory authorities when required by law
  • Service providers who assist in our operations

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access and review your personal information
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Withdraw consent for data processing
  • Request deletion of your data, subject to legal requirements

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. We will notify you of significant changes through our website or directly via email.

Rates & Fees

Loan Approval Policy

  • First-time loan applications take up to 7 business days for approval
  • Subsequent loans for customers with good repayment history are approved instantly
  • You can only have one active loan at a time

Loan Limits

  • Maximum loan amount is 333% of your savings balance
  • Your available limit is calculated as: (Maximum Limit) - (Current Loan Balance)
  • Example: With Ksh 150 savings (max Ksh 500 limit) and Ksh 200 current loan, your available limit is Ksh 300

Loan Products

Loan Amount Term Interest Rate Facility Fee
Ksh 1,000 - 10,000 30 days 7.8% per month Ksh 100
Ksh 10,001 - 30,000 60 days 6.5% per month Ksh 250
Ksh 30,001 - 60,000 90 days 5.9% per month Ksh 500

Other Charges

  • Late Payment: 5% of installment amount + Ksh 200 penalty
  • Early Repayment: No penalty
  • Loan Rescheduling: Ksh 300 administration fee
  • Default Fee: 10% of outstanding balance after 15 days overdue

Savings Account

  • Interest Rate: 5% per annum, compounded monthly
  • Minimum Balance: No minimum required
  • Withdrawal Fee: Ksh 50 per withdrawal

Example Calculation

For a Ksh 15,000 loan over 60 days:

  • Principal: Ksh 15,000
  • Interest: Ksh 15,000 × 6.5% × 2 months = Ksh 1,950
  • Facility Fee: Ksh 250
  • Total Repayable: Ksh 17,200

Note: Rates are subject to change based on market conditions and credit assessment.
